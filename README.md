# 🎓 Certificate & Career Tracker

The **Certificate & Career Tracker** is a simple, single-file web application that helps you **manage your professional certifications**.  
It allows you to track certificate names, issuing organizations, and expiry dates.  
The app also uses your certification categories to provide **personalized career path suggestions**, helping you visualize how your credentials can lead to new opportunities.

---

## ✨ Features
- 📝 **Add, Edit, and Delete Certificates** – Easily manage your professional credentials.  
- ⏰ **Expiry Tracking** – Certificates are automatically flagged as *Expiring Soon* or *Expired*.  
- 💼 **Personalized Career Suggestions** – Based on categories (e.g., IT, HR, Finance), get relevant career titles and descriptions.  
- 🔍 **Dynamic Filtering** – View certificates by status (*All, Expiring Soon, Expired*) or by career category.  
- 🖨 **Printable Ledger** – Generate a clean, printable report of your certificates.  
- 📱 **Responsive Design** – Mobile-first approach ensures smooth use across devices.
